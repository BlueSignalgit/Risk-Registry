Risk methodology

Risk score = Likelihood × Impact

Likelihood: 1 Rare, 2 Unlikely, 3 Possible, 4 Likely, 5 Almost Certain.

Impact: 1 Negligible, 2 Minor, 3 Moderate, 4 Major, 5 Severe.

Low 1–7 Medium 8–14

High 15–19 Critical 20–25

These illustrative inherent-risk scores are starting assumptions; validate each against real evidence before using operationally.
